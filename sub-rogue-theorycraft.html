Subtlety Rogue damage lab.

A hypothesis-driven look at builder damage, Shadow interactions, and the Season 2 tier set after the 12.1.5 damage correction. Optimized for testing new arena builds, not copying leaderboard talent choices.

Patch 12.1.5Season 2 · 2pc / 4pcExploratory / Not a proven BiS build13 OCT 2026
Research thesis:

Gloomblade could become an exceptionally efficient source of pressure outside Shadow Dance because it inherits the Season 2 builder bonus and bypasses armor. But the Shadow-damage double-dip correction may favor physical Backstab as a trigger for secondary Shadow events. The strongest build is whichever produces the most total damage per useful arena GCD without compromising Dance kill windows.

05 / Experimental design

A combat-log protocol that can answer it

Because builder damage can trigger secondary Shadow events, ordinary overall-DPS comparisons are too noisy to answer the mechanical question. Use identical gear and a consistent target, change one talent or effect at a time, and inspect the individual events.

01
Baseline Backstab with 2pc

Record direct hits, Energy spent, combo points, target mitigation and secondary Shadow events. Repeat from behind, front and against a different armor class.

02
Swap only the builder to Gloomblade

Keep hero talents and the rest of the offensive tree unchanged if possible. Compare damage per GCD and Energy, not just noncrit direct damage.

03
Introduce Mark / Unshakeable Drive

Measure a builder following Deathstalker's Mark consumption. Determine whether bonuses add or multiply and whether triggered events inherit them.

04
Isolate Shadow Blades & clone events

Aggregate damage caused by one builder GCD: direct ability hit + Shadow Blades + Goremaw's Bite or other relevant triggered events. Attribute by source, not only spell school.

05
Test Lingering Shadow timeline

Sample finishers at several delays after Dance; then open a second Dance before the original effect expires. Check persistence, refreshing and damage attribution.

06
Confirm arena practicality

Assess uptime, dangerous GCDs between stuns, defensives forced and the six-second kill-window damage. Don't trade meaningful kill pressure for harmless damage.

Primary outcome: total attributable damage per useful GCD and per Energy, under live PvP modifiers. Secondary outcome: whether any sustained-pressure gain is worth the talent cost and changes to your control / burst sequence.
